# Flare Social

Flare presents content and relationships from multiple social platforms through one shared product language.

## Language

**Post**:
A published social content item shown across supported platforms. Platform-specific language such as “note” is reserved for explicitly service-specific experiences.
_Avoid_: Status or toot as generic terms

**Repost**:
The generic act of redistributing another post. Service-specific experiences may use “Boost” for Mastodon or “Renote” for Misskey.
_Avoid_: Reblog or retweet as generic terms

**Favorite**:
A platform action that marks content as a favorite.
_Avoid_: Favourite

**Server**:
A remote social service endpoint a user connects to. “Instance” is reserved for service-specific contexts where it is the platform’s established term.
_Avoid_: Instance in generic setup copy
